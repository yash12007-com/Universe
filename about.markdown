# A universe is better when we explore it together.

**Yash12007 Universe** is a community journal for people who look up and ask questions. We share stories about galaxies, planets, the deep sky, new discoveries, and the experience of exploring space from right here on Earth.

## Have a story to share?

We welcome thoughtful contributions from astronomy enthusiasts, observers, photographers, and curious minds everywhere. You can write about a celestial object, share an observing report, explain a space-science idea, or tell us what the night sky means to you.

To propose a post, email [info@yash12007.com](mailto:info@yash12007.com) with a short pitch and a few lines about yourself. Please include image credits and sources for factual claims. We review submissions before publishing so the journal remains accurate, kind, and welcoming.

## Stay in orbit

Use the newsletter form on the [home page]({{ '/' | relative_url }}#newsletter) for occasional stories and sky notes. Newsletter signups need an email provider endpoint configured by the site owner; submissions are not stored by this Jekyll site itself.
